About Us

Football Brings Us Together

Kingston Sudanese Youth Club provides football for local youth to promote integration and education throughout football.

Open to every child

No fees, no trials — every session is free and welcomes all abilities.

Safe, trained coaching

All coaches are trained, vetted volunteers focused on wellbeing first.

Built by the community

Run for and with local families, on local pitches, for the long term.

What we do

Four ways we support children, young people and families in our community.

Football Coaching

Regular sessions that bring the community together through football — building fitness, friendship and teamwork.

Refugee Support

Advice and guidance to help refugees settle in and integrate confidently into the local community.

English & IT Skills

Basic English and IT classes for young people aged 14–24, building skills for school, work and everyday life.

Job Support

Hands-on help with CVs, job applications and finding volunteering opportunities to build experience.
