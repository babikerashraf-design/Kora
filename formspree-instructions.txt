HOW TO CONNECT THE CONTACT FORM TO FORMSPREE
==============================================

WHAT NEEDS TO CHANGE IN THE HTML
---------------------------------
Just one thing: the placeholder web address in the form's "action" line.

Open index.html and find this line (search for "formspree"):

    <form action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST">

Replace YOUR_FORM_ID with your real Formspree endpoint ID once you have
one (see steps below). Nothing else in the file needs to change.

Example — if Formspree gives you the ID "abcd1234", the line becomes:

    <form action="https://formspree.io/f/abcd1234" method="POST">

That's it. Save the file and the form is live.


STEPS TO GET YOUR FORMSPREE ID
--------------------------------
1. Go to https://formspree.io and click "Get Started" / "Sign Up".
   Free plan needs no card, just an email address.

2. Once logged in, click "New Form".

3. Give it a name (e.g. "KSYC Contact Form") and set the email address
   where you want submissions sent — this can be any inbox you check.

4. Formspree will show you an endpoint URL that looks like:
       https://formspree.io/f/abcd1234
   Copy the part after "/f/" — that's your Form ID.

5. Paste that ID into index.html as shown above.

6. Formspree will ask you to verify the form with a test submission the
   first time — just fill in the live contact form on your site once
   after publishing, and confirm the verification email Formspree sends.


THINGS TO KNOW
-----------------
- Free plan: up to 50 form submissions per month. Fine for a small
  community site; upgrade only if you outgrow it.

- Replies: the form already sends the visitor's typed email address to
  Formspree as the "email" field, so when you get the notification
  email, hitting Reply goes straight back to them — no extra setup.

- Spam protection: there's a hidden "honeypot" field already built into
  the form (invisible to real visitors). Formspree automatically
  discards any submission where it's been filled in, which is a common
  sign of a spam bot. No action needed on your part.

- Subject line: submissions arrive with the subject "New enquiry from
  Kingston Sudanese Youth Club website" already set, so they're easy to
  spot in your inbox.

- Optional upgrade later: by default, after someone submits the form
  they're redirected to a generic Formspree "thank you" page. If you'd
  rather they land back on your own site with a custom thank-you
  message, that needs one small addition (a hidden "_next" field) —
  just ask and it can be added once you're ready.
